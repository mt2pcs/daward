Cinematic 3D interface concept frame for a sports awards website (DAZN AWARDS 2026), 16:9.
Pure black infinite space. Visual language: real sports broadcast footage pieces as flat, thin, borderless panels floating in deep 3D space with strong perspective and shallow depth of field; a few flat translucent colored shards (magenta-red, coral, purple, cyan, lime, yellow) radiating from a far vanishing point, like the burst in the DAZN AWARDS logo.
Premium and cinematic, like a title sequence by a top motion-design studio. No neon glow, no bloom, no glowing lines, no sci-fi HUD clutter, no particles. Absolutely no text, letters or numbers anywhere (except broadcast scoreboards inside footage).
