SHOT: wide establishing shot. About 100 small 16:9 panels of different sports footage (rugby, baseball, soccer, basketball, boxing, motorsport, crowds) float in a vast cluster that recedes into deep black space like a galaxy of moments: the nearest panels are larger and sharp, far ones tiny and soft. Sparse colored shards radiate from the far center behind them. The viewer feels the scale of a whole year of sport, and that every panel is a world you could fly into.
