Style rules: photoreal cinematic key frame of a premium real-time 3D web experience, 2026 top-tier craft. Deep black space. The connections are drawn like diffusion-MRI tractography of a human brain connectome: tens of thousands of hair-thin, smooth, anti-aliased, translucent fibres gathered into thick curved bundles that fan out at their ends into individual strands. Fibres are soft warm white with faint colour picked up from the broadcast images they touch; density, not brightness, makes them visible. Depth of field: near fibres softly out of focus, far ones hazy.
ABSOLUTELY NO: glow, bloom, halos, neon, lens flares, light streaks, sparks, particles, dust, dots, nodes drawn as circles, grids, straight lines, UI panels, labels, text, logos, borders or frames around images, collage.

Scene (wide view of the whole): about one hundred dense clusters float at different depths in the black space; each cluster is a tight cloud of many tiny real sports broadcast stills (football, baseball, rugby, basketball, boxing) — one cluster is one moment of the year. Between the clusters run thick, gently curving bundles of fibres, so the whole year of sport reads as one living organism, one nervous system. Bundles are thicker where clusters share more. Some bundles pass close to the camera huge and soft, most recede into the dark. Asymmetric composition, low camera, sense of immense quantity.
