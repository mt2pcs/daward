Style rules: photoreal cinematic key frame of a premium real-time 3D web experience, 2026 top-tier craft (think high-end scientific visualisation exhibited as art). Deep black space with faint atmospheric depth.
The connections look like diffusion-MRI tractography of a brain connectome rendered as silk: hundreds of thousands of extremely fine, translucent, anti-aliased filaments, each thinner than a human hair, gathered into smooth curved tracts. A tract is only visible because so many filaments overlap; where it fans out you can see single filaments. Filaments are NOT cables, NOT rope, NOT yarn, NOT noodles: they are near-invisible individually, silky, with a soft sheen. Each filament takes a faint tint from the two images it connects (soft grass greens, skin tones, kit colours) over a warm-white base. Atmospheric perspective: far tracts fade into the black.
ABSOLUTELY NO: glow, bloom, halos, neon, lens flares, light streaks, sparks, particles, dust, dots, circles, grids, straight lines, UI panels, labels, text, logos, borders or frames around images, collage, duplicated images.
Scene (wide view of the whole): about one hundred dense clusters float at different depths in the black space; each cluster is an organic, loosely spherical swarm of many tiny real sports broadcast stills floating at slightly different depths (not a flat pile) (football, baseball, rugby, basketball, boxing) — one cluster is one moment of the year. Between the clusters run thick, gently curving bundles of fibres, so the whole year of sport reads as one living organism, one nervous system. Bundles are thicker where clusters share more. Some bundles pass close to the camera huge and soft, most recede into the dark. Asymmetric composition, low camera, sense of immense quantity.
