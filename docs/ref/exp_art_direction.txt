Art direction (strict): the interactive experience of "DAZN 100 MOMENTS" (DAZN AWARDS 2026). The DAZN AWARDS logo motif: a small white outlined square frame at the vanishing point, with straight light shards radiating outward like an explosion. We are INSIDE that burst: a deep 3D tunnel of straight radiating rays converging to the white square far ahead. Pure black space, rays in electric yellow (#EBFF00), white, magenta and blue, some rays are flat tapered glass-like shards. Real broadcast video stills of many sports (Japanese pro baseball, World Cup soccer, B.League basketball, rugby, boxing, motor racing) float as 16:9 cards ALONG the rays at different depths: near cards large at the edges, far cards small near the vanishing point, cards tilted with the perspective of the ray they ride. Typography: Japanese headline labels painted like fluorescent highlighter marker strokes (diagonal, 3 layered strokes) in the ray's color with heavy black bold Japanese text on top. Small UI text in white condensed uppercase. No rounded bubbles, no glassmorphism panels, no cartoon. Cinematic, premium, dense but readable. This is a pixel-accurate UI design mockup of a real web experience.
